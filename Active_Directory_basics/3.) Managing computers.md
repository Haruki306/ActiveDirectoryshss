<h2>So all machines that join a domain (except for domain controllers) will be put into a container called "Computers"</h2>
<img width="860" height="515" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8d3f84f9-7d73-472d-8da2-30823a6f7908" />
<h3>Having all of our devices there is not neat and since it is very likely that there are different policies from different groups what we can do is segregate the devices according to their use.</h3>
<h3>We can divide these devices to at least 3 common categories:</h3>
<h4>Workstations</h4>
<h4>Servers</h4>
<h4>Domain Controllers</h4>

<h3>We can create them by right clicking on the tree > New > Organizational Unit</h3>
<img width="819" height="761" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b0bed3d8-ab75-4ccb-8ac6-1dd94540ed8e" />
<h3>then just name it to the category that we want and click ok, Now we have an OU for the workstation then just repeat for the **servers** since we already have **Domain Controllers** we will not be doing it to that OU</h3>
